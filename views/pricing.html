The small print, in plain words

  • Prices are workshop labour. Engine oil, filters, shims, gaskets, pads and other parts are extra and shown line by line.
  • "from" means the job varies by model. For example, shim work on an inline-four takes longer than on a twin. You get the exact figure on your estimate.
  • "On quote" jobs, such as engine rebuilds and ECU remaps, depend on what we find or on the partner tuner's fee. We quote them after inspection.
  • Seized or damaged fasteners, rust, or previous bad repairs can add time. We'll call you before doing anything that changes the estimate.
  • Bring your own parts? We'll fit them at the same labour rate. Workmanship warranty covers our work, not the part.
